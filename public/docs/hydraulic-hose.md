# Hydraulic Brake Hose & Fittings

## Hose Outer Diameters

| Brand | Hose OD | Fitting Type |
|---|---|---|
| Shimano | 5 mm | Banjo bolt + olive + insert |
| SRAM/Avid | 5 mm | Barbed fitting + olive |
| Magura | 5 mm | Banjo bolt (proprietary) |
| TRP | 5 mm | Olive + insert |
| Formula | 5.5 mm | Olive + insert |

## Fitting Components

### Shimano
- **Olive** (compression ring): 6 mm OD — olive compresses onto hose
- **Insert** (barb): slides inside hose end
- **Banjo bolt**: single or double banjo at caliper end
- Torque: **6 Nm** (banjo), **5 Nm** (lever fitting)

### SRAM
- **Olive** + **barbed insert**
- DOT 4 or DOT 5.1 fluid
- Torque: **7 Nm** (caliper fitting)

## Hose Cutting & Bleeding

1. Cut hose square with a clean blade — no fraying
2. Insert fitting components before cutting to final length
3. Bleed from caliper up to lever (mineral oil) or lever down to caliper (DOT)
4. Shimano recommends TL-BH10 hose insert tool

## Hose Length Tips

- Route before cutting — allow for full handlebar rotation
- Add 20 mm for future frame swaps
- Internal routing: measure actual routed length, not straight-line

## Fluid Change Intervals

| Condition | Interval |
|---|---|
| Normal use | Every 2 years or 5,000 km |
| Wet/heavy use | Every 1 year |
| DOT fluid (hygroscopic) | 2 years maximum |
