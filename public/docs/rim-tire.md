# Rim & Tire Width Compatibility

## ETRTO Rim / Tire Width Chart

Rim internal width (IRW) determines minimum and recommended tire width:

| IRW (mm) | Min Tire Width | Recommended | Max Tire Width |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | 18 mm | 23–25 mm | 28 mm |
| 15 | 23 mm | 25–28 mm | 32 mm |
| 17 | 25 mm | 28–35 mm | 45 mm |
| 19 | 28 mm | 32–45 mm | 50 mm |
| 21 | 32 mm | 38–50 mm | 55 mm |
| 23 | 37 mm | 45–57 mm | 62 mm |
| 25 | 42 mm | 50–60 mm | 65 mm |

## Hookless Rim Restrictions

Hookless (hook-free bead seats) require:

- **Tubeless only** — no tubed clinchers on hookless
- Max pressure: **72.5 PSI (5 bar)** — even for road tires
- Tire must be ETRTO-marked as hookless-compatible
- Minimum tire width: 28 mm on hookless rims

> Brands with hookless rims: ENVE, Zipp (454/353), Reynolds, Roval, DT Swiss, Reserve

## Tubeless Compatibility

Both rim and tire must be tubeless-rated:
- Rim: tubeless-ready (sealed bed, no spoke holes exposed)
- Tire: tubeless-ready (airtight casing, no inner tube needed)
- Sealant: 30–60 ml depending on tire volume
