# Axle & Hub Standards

## Front Axle Standards

| Standard | Diameter | Length | Thread | Common Frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| QR (Quick Release) | 9 mm | 100 mm | M5 nut | Traditional road/MTB |
| 15 mm Thru-Axle | 15 mm | 100 mm | M15×1.5 or P1.50 | MTB front |
| 12 mm Thru-Axle | 12 mm | 100 mm | M12×1.0 or 1.5 | Road disc, gravel |
| 20 mm (Boost) | 20 mm | 110 mm | M20 | Downhill, freeride |

## Rear Axle Standards

| Standard | Width | Diameter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| QR Road | 130 mm | 9 mm | Traditional road |
| QR MTB | 135 mm | 9 mm | Traditional MTB |
| 12×142 mm | 142 mm | 12 mm | Road disc standard |
| 12×148 mm Boost | 148 mm | 12 mm | Modern MTB — wider Q-factor |
| 12×157 mm Super Boost | 157 mm | 12 mm | Downhill / e-bike |
| 12×141 mm (SRAM UDH) | 141 mm | 12 mm | SRAM Transmission AXS |

## Boost vs Standard

Boost (110/148) adds 6 mm to front and rear spacing, allowing:
- Wider hub flange → stronger wheel build
- Better chainline for 1× MTB drivetrains
- Incompatible with non-Boost frames without adapters

## Thru-Axle Thread Pitches

Different manufacturers use different pitches — check before cross-installing:

| Brand | Front Thread | Rear Thread |
|---|---|---|
| Shimano | M12×1.5 | M12×1.5 |
| SRAM | M12×1.0 | M12×1.0 |
| Specialized | M15×1.5 (front) | M12×1.5 |
| Trek | M15×P1.50 | M12×1.75 |
