# Handlebar Clamp Diameters

## Clamp Diameter Standards

| Diameter | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 22.2 mm | Cruiser, BMX, old MTB | Narrow — being phased out |
| 25.4 mm (1") | Road drop bars (older) | Classic road |
| 26.0 mm | Rare, some older MTB | Avoid — hard to source |
| 31.8 mm (OS) | Road drops, MTB flat bars | **Most common modern** |
| 35 mm | Modern MTB flat/riser bars | Stiffer, heavier |

## Stem Clamp Compatibility

Stem clamp diameter **must match** handlebar center diameter exactly. No adaptation is safe — wrong diameter risks handlebar failure.

## Road Drop Bar Widths

| Width (c-c) | Rider Shoulder Width | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 36–38 cm | < 35 cm | Small/narrow |
| 40 cm | 35–38 cm | Medium |
| 42–44 cm | 38–42 cm | Standard |
| 46+ cm | > 42 cm | Large/wide |

## MTB Bar Width & Rise

| Discipline | Typical Width | Rise |
|---|---|---|
| XC | 680–720 mm | 0–15 mm |
| Trail | 720–760 mm | 15–30 mm |
| Enduro | 760–800 mm | 20–40 mm |
| DH | 800–820 mm | 30–50 mm |
