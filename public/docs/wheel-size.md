# Wheel Size Standards

## ISO / ETRTO Bead Seat Diameter

| Common Name | ETRTO BSD | Actual OD (typical) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 700c (road) | 622 mm | ~680–700 mm | Road, gravel, most hybrids |
| 650b / 27.5" | 584 mm | ~640–650 mm | Modern MTB, wide-tire gravel |
| 26" (MTB) | 559 mm | ~610–620 mm | Older MTB, dirt jump |
| 29" / 700c MTB | 622 mm | ~735–740 mm | Modern MTB, gravel |
| 650c | 571 mm | ~630 mm | Small road frames, triathlon |
| 20" (BMX) | 406 mm | ~460 mm | BMX, folding bikes |
| 24" | 507 mm | ~565 mm | Kids, trials |

## Notes

- **700c and 29"** share the same BSD (622 mm) — same rim, different tires
- **650b and 27.5"** are identical — just different naming conventions (road vs MTB)
- **Mixed wheel** setups (29" front / 27.5" rear) are used in some trail MTBs
- Tire outer diameter varies by tire width — 700c × 23 mm ≠ 700c × 50 mm in OD
