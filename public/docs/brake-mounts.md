# Brake Mount Standards

## Disc Brake Frame/Fork Mounts

| Standard | Abbreviation | Bolt Spacing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| International Standard | IS | 74 mm (front) / 51 mm (rear) | Older MTB standard |
| Post Mount | PM | 74 mm (front) / 51 mm (rear) | Most common MTB |
| Flat Mount | FM | 28 mm | Modern road + gravel disc |
| Center Lock (rear) | — | — | Shimano-specific rotor attachment |

## Flat Mount Details

- Road standard since ~2015 — slimmer, lighter
- Direct bolt-on for 140 mm and 160 mm rotors
- Requires adapter for 180 mm+
- Shimano, SRAM, TRP all use flat mount

## Post Mount to Flat Mount Adapters

| Conversion | Adapter Size |
|---|---|
| PM → FM | Shimano SM-MA-F140P/D or equivalent |
| IS 74 → PM 203 | +43 mm front adapter |
| IS 51 → PM 180 | +43 mm rear adapter |

## Rim Brake Mounts

| Standard | Pivot Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (long reach) | 5 mm M5 | Road bikes, 49–59 mm |
| Short reach | 5 mm M5 | Road, 39–49 mm |
| Mini-V | 5 mm M5 | Cyclocross |
| V-Brake (linear pull) | 5 mm M5 | MTB — 110 mm arm |
