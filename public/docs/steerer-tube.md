# Fork & Steerer Tube Standards

## Steerer Diameters

| Standard | OD | Wall Thickness (typical) | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1" | 25.4 mm | 2 mm (steel) | Steel |
| 1 1/8" | 28.6 mm | 2 mm (alloy), 1 mm (carbon) | Alloy, Carbon |
| 1.5" | 38.1 mm | 2 mm | Alloy, Carbon |
| Tapered | 28.6 mm top / 38.1 mm bottom | Varies | Carbon, Alloy |

## Carbon Steerer Rules

- Never cut carbon steerer without a carbon-specific cutting guide
- Use a star nut alternative (expansion plug) — never a traditional star nut in carbon
- Torque stem clamp bolts to spec: typically 5–6 Nm (check stem spec)
- Minimum insertion depth into stem: 2.5× steerer OD (e.g., 70 mm for 1 1/8")

## Preload

Stem cap bolt pre-loads the headset — it does **not** clamp the steerer. Tighten until play disappears, then clamp stem bolts. Over-tightening the cap bolt damages bearings.

## Rake & Trail

| Geometry | Effect |
|---|---|
| More rake | More trail → slower steering |
| Less rake | Less trail → quicker steering |
| Trail (mm) = (wheel radius × cos(HA) − fork rake) / sin(HA) | — |

Where HA = head angle in degrees.
