# Headset Standards

## Overview

The headset is the bearing assembly connecting the fork steerer to the frame head tube. Standards define head tube inner diameter, cup press diameter, and steerer diameter.

## Standards Table

| Standard | Head Tube ID | Cup OD | Steerer | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EC34 (1") | 34 mm | 30 mm | 1" | Vintage |
| EC44 (1 1/8") | 44 mm | 40 mm | 1 1/8" | Classic threadless |
| IS42 (1 1/8") | 42 mm | press-in | 1 1/8" | Integrated shell |
| IS52 (1.5") | 52 mm | press-in | 1.5" | Modern road/gravel |
| ZS44 (1 1/8") | 44 mm | zero-stack | 1 1/8" | Road compact |
| ZS56 (1.5") | 56 mm | zero-stack | 1.5" | Road/gravel tapered |
| IS41/IS52 tapered | 41.8 / 52 mm | press-in | 1 1/8"→1.5" tapered | Modern MTB |
| EC44/IS52 | 44 / 52 mm | mixed | Tapered | Many current road frames |

## Steerer Tube Diameters

| Standard | OD | Frame Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1" (25.4 mm) | 25.4 mm | Vintage, kids |
| 1 1/8" (28.6 mm) | 28.6 mm | Classic road/MTB |
| 1.5" (38.1 mm) | 38.1 mm | DH, some MTB |
| Tapered 1 1/8"→1.5" | Both | Modern road + MTB |

## Cane Creek Naming Convention

- **EC** = External Cup (cup sits outside head tube)
- **IS** = Internal / Integrated (cup pressed into head tube)
- **ZS** = Zero Stack (sits flush with head tube top)
- Number = nominal head tube inner diameter
