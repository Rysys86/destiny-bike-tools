# Disc Brake Rotor Standards

## Rotor Sizes

| Size | Use | Stopping Power |
|---|---|---|
| 140 mm | Road, light gravel | Adequate for flat terrain |
| 160 mm | Road disc, XC MTB | Standard road/XC |
| 180 mm | Trail MTB, gravel | More power |
| 203 mm | Enduro, DH MTB | Maximum |
| 220 mm | E-bike, tandem | High heat capacity |

## Attachment Standards

| Standard | Bolts | Compatible Hubs |
|---|---|---|
| 6-bolt (ISO) | 6× T25 Torx M5 | Universal |
| Centerlock | Lockring | Shimano, some DT Swiss |
| Centerlock w/ adapter | 6-bolt adapter | Convert centerlock to 6-bolt |

Torque for 6-bolt: **4 Nm** (steel rotor) — follow rotor manufacturer spec.

## Hydraulic Fluid Types

| Fluid | Brands | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mineral Oil | Shimano, Magura | Non-hygroscopic, gentle on paint |
| DOT 4 / DOT 5.1 | SRAM, Avid, Formula, TRP | Hygroscopic — absorb moisture over time |

> **Never mix DOT and mineral oil** — seal damage and brake failure result.

## Rotor Bedding Procedure

1. 20 moderate stops from 30 km/h
2. 10 hard stops from 30 km/h
3. Allow to cool between sets — never drag brakes during cool-down

## Rotor Wear Limit

| Size | New Thickness | Min Thickness |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 1.8–2.0 mm | 1.5 mm |
| Heavy-duty | 2.0–2.3 mm | 1.8 mm |
