# Seatpost Diameter Standards

## Common Diameters

| Diameter | Common Frames |
|---|---|
| 22.2 mm | Kids, BMX |
| 25.4 mm | Older road, steel MTB |
| 26.8 mm | Some steel frames |
| 27.2 mm | Most road bikes, steel MTB |
| 28.6 mm | Some Specialized, Cannondale |
| 30.0 mm | Some alloy MTB |
| 30.9 mm | MTB (medium) |
| 31.6 mm | Most carbon road, MTB |
| 34.9 mm | Older large MTB |
| 36.4 mm | Some freeride, downhill |

> Always measure seat tube inner diameter before buying — fractions of a mm matter. A 0.2 mm undersized post will slip.

## Dropper Post Sizing

Same diameter list applies. Additional considerations:
- **Travel**: 60–200 mm depending on frame clearance
- **Minimum insertion**: post must bottom to minimum mark
- **Routing**: internal vs external — check frame port availability

## Material Notes

| Material | Feel | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alloy | Stiff | Heavier | Durable, affordable |
| Carbon | Vibration damping | Lighter | Check clamp torque (5–6 Nm typical) |
| Titanium | Flex/comfort | Medium | Ideal for long rides |
